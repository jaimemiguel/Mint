Google gives you a wide range of customization options so your workspace feels like it was built for your team. From toggling dark mode and adjusting font sizes to uploading your company logo and setting a custom welcome message for new members, you can shape both the visual identity and the day-to-day layout of your workspace to fit how you actually work. Most appearance settings apply to your personal account only, while branding and domain options affect every member.

<Note>
  Workspace branding options — including logo, color scheme, and welcome message — require an **Admin** or **Owner** role. Personal appearance settings are available to all members.
</Note>

---

## Themes and Appearance

Control the look and feel of your personal workspace view under **Settings → Appearance**.

**Light and Dark Mode**
Switch between light and dark interface themes by toggling the **Appearance** selector. You can also set it to **System Default** to automatically match your operating system's current mode — Google will update in real time whenever your OS theme changes.

**Custom Color Accents**
Choose a primary accent color to personalize interactive elements like buttons, links, and focus indicators throughout the interface. Navigate to **Settings → Appearance → Accent Color**, pick a color from the palette or enter a hex value, and click **Apply**. This setting affects only your personal view.

**Font Size and Density**
Adjust how much information fits on screen at once:

- **Font Size** — Choose from Small, Medium (default), or Large to match your reading preference.
- **Display Density** — Select Compact, Comfortable, or Spacious to control the spacing between items in lists, tables, and sidebars. Compact is ideal for power users who want to see more content at a glance; Spacious works well on large displays or for users who prefer more breathing room.

## Workspace Branding

Workspace branding lets Admins create a cohesive, on-brand experience for every member of the organization. Access these settings under **Settings → Branding**.

**Upload a Workspace Logo**
Replace the default Google logo with your own organization's mark. Click **Upload Logo** and select an image file (PNG or SVG recommended, minimum 128×128 px). The logo appears in the top-left navigation bar and in outbound email notifications sent from your workspace.

**Set a Workspace Color Scheme**
Define the primary color that anchors your workspace's visual identity. This affects the top navigation bar, primary buttons, and other branded surfaces. Enter a hex color value or use the color picker to choose your color, then click **Save**. Choose a color with sufficient contrast against white text to maintain readability.

**Custom Welcome Message**
Write a message that new members see the first time they log in to your workspace. Use this space to greet new teammates, point them to onboarding resources, or share team norms. Navigate to **Settings → Branding → Welcome Message**, enter your message in the text field (Markdown is supported), and click **Save**.

## Layout Preferences

Tailor the structure of your workspace interface to match the way your team navigates and works. Layout preferences are personal by default and do not affect other members.

**Sidebar Configuration**
Collapse or expand the left-hand sidebar to reclaim screen space when you're focused on a single task. Click the **⟨** arrow at the bottom of the sidebar to collapse it to an icon-only view. Your preference is saved automatically and persists across sessions.

You can also reorder sidebar sections by dragging items up or down. Pin your most-used sections to the top so they're always within reach.

**Dashboard Widget Arrangement**
Your home dashboard supports a customizable grid of widgets — recent activity, upcoming tasks, quick links, and more. To rearrange widgets:
1. Click the **Customize Dashboard** button in the top-right corner of the dashboard.
2. Drag widgets to your preferred position.
3. Click **Done** to save your layout.

To add or remove widgets, click **Add Widget** in customize mode and toggle the ones you want to show or hide.

**Default Views Per Section**
Each major section — such as Projects, Files, and Messages — lets you set a default view (list, grid, board, or calendar). Open the section, click the view selector in the toolbar, choose your preferred view, and select **Set as Default**. Google remembers your preference each time you return to that section.

## Custom Domains

You can serve your Google workspace from a custom domain (for example, `workspace.yourcompany.com`) to give members a seamless, branded access URL. Domain setup involves DNS configuration and SSL provisioning. For full step-by-step instructions, visit the [Google Help Center](https://support.google.com) or contact your workspace administrator.
