Your Google account is the central hub that connects you to every product and service in the Google suite. Whether you're collaborating on documents, managing cloud storage, or integrating with Google's APIs, your account gives you a single, secure place to control how you access and use everything Google has to offer.

## What's in Your Account

Your account brings together all the essential tools you need to stay in control of your Google experience. From one place, you can manage:

- **Billing & Subscriptions** — View your current plan, review past invoices, update payment methods, and upgrade or downgrade your subscription at any time.
- **Linked Services** — See every Google product and third-party application connected to your account, and revoke access whenever you choose.
- **Security Settings** — Monitor active sessions, enable two-factor authentication, rotate API keys, and review your login history to keep your account secure.
- **API Keys** — Generate, manage, and restrict API keys that allow your applications to authenticate with Google's services programmatically.

## Account Dashboard

When you sign in, the Account Dashboard gives you an at-a-glance view of your entire Google account. Here's what you'll find:

- **Recent Activity** — A chronological feed of actions taken across your linked products, including logins, file edits, and API calls, so you can quickly spot anything unexpected.
- **Linked Products** — A summary of every Google service connected to your account, with quick links to jump directly into each one.
- **Usage Stats** — Real-time metrics on storage consumption, API call volume, and active user seats, helping you understand how you're using Google's resources and plan accordingly.

Use the dashboard as your starting point whenever you need to audit your account, troubleshoot an issue, or explore a new Google service.
