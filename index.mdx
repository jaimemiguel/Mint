## Get Started in Four Steps

Follow this onboarding flow to go from zero to fully set up with Google's platform.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create Your Account">
    Sign up at [accounts.google.com](https://accounts.google.com) to get access to the full suite of Google products and services. All you need is an email address to get started.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Authenticate">
    Generate an API key from the [Google Developer Console](https://console.developers.google.com) and configure authentication for the products and services you want to use.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Explore Features">
    Browse Google Workspace, Google Cloud, and the broader product ecosystem to discover the tools that fit your workflow — from Gmail and Drive to Cloud Functions and BigQuery.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Build with the API">
    Use your API key to make your first call, integrate Google's services into your application, and automate the workflows that matter most to your team.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Need help? Visit the [Google Help Center](https://support.google.com) or reach out through the developer community forums. You can also check the [API Reference](/api/introduction) for detailed endpoint documentation and code examples.
</Tip>
