Google brings together everything your team needs to communicate, collaborate, and get work done — all in one place. Whether you're co-editing a document with a colleague across the world, automating repetitive tasks through integrations, or staying on top of activity with smart notifications, every feature is designed to keep you and your team moving forward.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Collaboration" icon="users" href="/features/collaboration">
    Invite teammates, share workspaces, and co-edit documents in real time. Manage permissions and track activity across your entire team.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Integrations" icon="plug" href="/features/integrations">
    Connect Google to Slack, GitHub, Zapier, Salesforce, and hundreds of other services to keep your tools in sync and automate your workflows.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Notifications" icon="bell" href="/features/notifications">
    Stay informed with email, in-app, and push notifications. Configure exactly what you hear about and when, so nothing important slips through.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Customization" icon="sliders" href="/configuration/customization">
    Tailor Google to fit the way your team works — custom fields, themes, workflows, and more.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

Each feature in Google is built to complement the others. Your integrations feed activity into your notifications, your notifications surface changes made by collaborators, and your customization settings shape how all of it is presented to you. The result is a platform that adapts to your workflow rather than the other way around.

<Tip>
  You can automate almost any workflow in Google using the API. From triggering actions based on workspace events to syncing data with external systems, the API gives you full programmatic control. See the [API Reference](/api/introduction) to get started.
</Tip>
