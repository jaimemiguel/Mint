Google is built for teams. Every feature in the platform is designed to make it easy to work alongside other people — whether you're onboarding a new colleague, co-editing a proposal with your manager, or keeping an eye on what your team is shipping. With shared workspaces, real-time editing, and a live activity feed, everyone stays aligned without the overhead of constant check-ins.

## Inviting Team Members

Getting your team into Google takes just a few steps. You can invite anyone using their email address and control exactly what they can see and do from the moment they join.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open your workspace">
    Navigate to the workspace you want to share from your Google dashboard.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Share or Invite">
    Select the **Share** button in the top-right corner of the workspace, or open **Workspace Settings** and choose the **Members** tab.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter email addresses">
    Type one or more email addresses in the invitation field. You can add multiple recipients at once by separating addresses with a comma.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set a permission level">
    Choose the appropriate role for each invitee:
    - **Viewer** — can read content but cannot make changes
    - **Editor** — can create, edit, and delete content
    - **Admin** — full control, including managing members and settings
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send the invite">
    Click **Send Invite**. Each recipient will receive an email with a link to join the workspace. Pending invitations appear under the **Members** tab until accepted.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Shared Workspaces

A workspace is the primary container for your team's work in Google. It holds documents, projects, files, and conversations — everything organized in one place so your team always knows where to look. You can create as many workspaces as you need, making it easy to separate work by team, project, client, or any other structure that fits your organization.

Within a workspace, members see the same content and contribute to a shared history. Permissions you set at the workspace level apply by default to everything inside, though you can override them on individual resources when needed.

## Real-Time Editing

When two or more people open the same document, Google automatically switches into collaborative editing mode. You'll see each collaborator's cursor in a distinct color along with their name, so you always know who is editing what.

Changes appear instantly for all participants — there's no need to refresh or manually sync. If two people edit the same section simultaneously, Google uses operational transformation to intelligently merge changes so nothing is lost. A full version history is maintained automatically, and you can restore any previous version at any time from the **History** panel.

## Activity Feed

The activity feed gives you a running log of everything happening in your workspace. Every edit, comment, share, and status change is recorded and surfaced in chronological order so you can catch up at a glance.

You can filter the feed by member, resource type, or date range to focus on what matters most. Clicking any activity item takes you directly to the relevant resource, so following up on a teammate's work is always one click away.

<Note>
  Permission levels determine what members can access across the entire workspace. A **Viewer** cannot edit or comment, an **Editor** can create and modify content but cannot manage members, and an **Admin** has full control including the ability to remove members and delete the workspace. Review permissions carefully before inviting external collaborators such as clients or contractors.
</Note>
